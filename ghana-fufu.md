Peel the cassava and plantains.
Wash them thoroughly.
Cut them into medium-sized pieces.
Remove the tough central fibre from the cassava if necessary.
Step 2: Boil the ingredients
Put the cassava and plantain pieces in a pot.
Add enough water to cover them.
Boil for approximately 20–30 minutes, or until they are very soft.
Drain the water.
Make sure both ingredients are soft enough to mash easily.

Step 3: Pound the fufu
Transfer the cooked cassava and plantain into a large mortar.
Pound them with a pestle while turning and folding the mixture.
Continue pounding until the mixture becomes smooth, stretchy, and free of lumps.
Add a tiny amount of warm water if necessary to soften it.
Step 4: Shape and serve
Wet your hands with a little water.
Shape the fufu into medium-sized balls.
Serve warm with your favourite Ghanaian soup.
Tip: If you don't have a mortar and pestle, you can mash the boiled ingredients thoroughly and knead them while still hot. A strong food processor designed for dough can also help.

🇬🇭 A little secret for Ghana-style fufu
The secret is to cook the cassava and green plantain until very soft, then pound them thoroughly. The mixture should be smooth, stretchy, and not grainy.
