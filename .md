Ingredients
2 cups of corn flour or finely ground corn
4–5 cups of water
Sugar to taste (optional)
A pinch of salt
Ginger (optional)
Method
Mix the corn flour with a little cold water to make a smooth paste.
Bring the remaining water to a boil in a pot.
Gradually pour the corn mixture into the boiling water while stirring continuously.
Reduce the heat and cook for about 10–15 minutes, stirring regularly.
Add a pinch of salt and sugar if desired.
Add a little hot water if the koko is too thick.
Serve hot with puff-puff, bread, or beans cake.
2. Millet Koko (Spiced Koko) 🌾

This version is especially delicious when you want a warm, filling breakfast.

Ingredients
2 cups of millet flour
5 cups of water
1 small piece of fresh ginger
A pinch of cloves (optional)
Sugar to taste
A pinch of salt
Method
Mix the millet flour with cold water until smooth.
Blend or crush the ginger and cloves, then add them to the mixture.
Bring some water to a boil in a pot.
Slowly add the millet mixture while stirring continuously.
Cook for 15–20 minutes over low to medium heat.
Add sugar and salt according to your taste.
Serve hot.
Tip: You can serve millet koko with milk, roasted groundnuts, or puff-puff.

3. Fermented Corn Koko (Sour Koko) 🥣
This is a traditional style of koko with a slightly sour taste, made using fermented corn.

Ingredients
2 cups of fermented corn dough or fermented corn flour
4–5 cups of water
Sugar to taste
A pinch of salt
Method
Put the fermented corn dough into a bowl.
Add some cold water and mix until smooth.
Strain the mixture if you want a smoother koko.
Bring water to a boil in a pot.
Pour in the corn mixture gradually, stirring continuously.
Allow it to cook for 10–15 minutes, stirring to prevent lumps.
Add sugar if desired and serve hot.
Tip: Fermentation gives the koko its characteristic tangy taste.
