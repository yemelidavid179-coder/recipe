5 fresh tomatoes
2 tablespoons tomato paste
1 large onion
1 red bell pepper
2–3 hot peppers, to taste
½ cup vegetable oil
2 cloves of garlic
1 small piece of ginger
4–5 cups chicken stock or water, as needed
2 seasoning cubes (optional)
Salt and curry powder to taste
Cooked chicken or beef (optional)
Preparation
Blend the tomatoes, bell pepper, hot pepper, garlic, and ginger.
Heat the oil in a pot and fry the chopped onion.
Add the tomato paste and fry for 3–5 minutes.
Pour in the blended mixture and cook for 15–20 minutes until the sauce thickens and loses its raw tomato taste.
Add the seasoning, salt, and chicken stock.
Wash the rice and add it to the sauce. Stir well.
Cover the pot and cook over low heat until the rice is tender, adding a little water if necessary.
Serve with chicken, fried plantain, salad, or shito.
Secret: Cook the rice over low heat once the liquid has been added to help it absorb the tomato sauce without burning.

Delicious Waakye: A Classic Ghanaian Dish
Waakye is rice cooked with beans and traditionally sorghum leaves, which give it its characteristic reddish-brown colour.

Main ingredients
2 cups rice
1 cup black-eyed peas or cowpeas
Water
Sorghum leaves, if available
Salt to taste
Preparation
Wash the beans and cook them until partly tender.
Add the sorghum leaves and allow the water to develop colour.
Add the washed rice and enough water to cook everything.
