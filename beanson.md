2 cups Ghana rice
1 cup black-eyed beans or red beans
1 onion (chopped)
2 tomatoes
2 tablespoons vegetable oil
2 cloves garlic
Salt to taste
1 teaspoon ginger
Water
Preparation
1. Cook the beans

Wash the beans thoroughly.
Put them in a pot with enough water.
Boil until they are almost soft.
2. Add the rice

Wash the rice and add it to the pot of beans.
Add salt and enough water.
Stir gently and cover.
3. Cook together

Allow the rice and beans to cook on low heat until tender.
Add a little hot water if necessary.
Cook until the water dries and everything is soft.
🍅 Simple tomato stew
Heat vegetable oil in a saucepan.
Add chopped onions and garlic.
Add blended tomatoes and ginger.
Add salt and seasoning.
Cook until the oil separates from the tomato mixture.
