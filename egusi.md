🍲 Egusi Sauce (African Melon Seed Sauce)
Egusi soup is a popular West African dish made from ground melon seeds, palm oil, tomatoes, peppers, and seasonings. It is especially popular in Nigeria and is also enjoyed in Cameroon and other parts of West and Central Africa.


🥘 Ingredients
For a delicious pot of egusi sauce, you will need:

2 cups ground egusi (melon seeds)
½ cup red palm oil
2–3 fresh tomatoes (optional)
1–2 onions
Fresh pepper or scotch bonnet, to taste
Beef, cow skin (canda), fish, or smoked fish
Spinach, bitterleaf, pumpkin leaves, or other leafy vegetables
2 seasoning cubes (optional)
Salt to taste
Water or meat stock
👩‍🍳 How to Prepare Egusi Sauce
Cook your meat: Season the meat with salt, onion, and seasoning cubes. Cook until tender, keeping the stock.
Prepare the egusi: Mix the ground egusi with a little water to form a thick paste, or leave it dry if you prefer the frying method.
Heat the palm oil: Pour palm oil into a pot and heat gently. Add chopped onions and stir.
Add the egusi: Add the ground egusi and stir for a few minutes. Allow it to cook and form small lumps.
Add the tomatoes and pepper: If using them, blend or chop the tomatoes and pepper, then add to the pot.
Add meat and stock: Put in your cooked meat, fish, and a little stock. Stir gently and allow to simmer for 10–15 minutes.
Add vegetables: Stir in your chopped leafy vegetables and cook for another 3–5 minutes.
Taste and serve: Adjust the salt and seasoning, then serve hot.
🍽️ What to Eat with Egusi Sauce
Egusi goes well with:

Water fufu
Garri (eba)
Pounded yam
Boiled rice
Plantain
Corn fufu
😋 Tips for Delicious Egusi
Use good-quality melon seeds for a rich flavour.
Don't add too much water at once; egusi should be thick and rich.
Smoked fish adds a wonderful traditional flavour.
