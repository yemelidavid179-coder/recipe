2 cups corn flour (maize flour)
3–4 cups water
Preparation
Put 1 cup of corn flour in a bowl.
Add some cold water and mix into a smooth paste without lumps.
Boil 2 cups of water in a pot.
Pour the corn paste into the boiling water while stirring.
Gradually add the remaining corn flour.
Stir continuously with a wooden spatula until thick and smooth.
Add a little water if it becomes too hard.
Cover and allow it to steam for about 5–10 minutes.
Turn and knead until smooth and stretchy.
